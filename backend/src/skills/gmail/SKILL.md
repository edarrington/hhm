# Household Gmail Skill

## Index
Google Gmail: manage household email, compose and send messages, search inbox, create drafts.

## Base
# Email Operations

- Treat email like helping the household communicate, not managing a database.
- Summarize email summaries before sharing full content.
- Always confirm before sending emails on behalf of the household.
- Be aware of household email preferences and communication style.

## Pack
## Household Gmail Tools

You're helping the household manage their shared email communication.

### Reading Email
- Summarize key points instead of reading entire emails.
- Ask what specific emails to focus on if the inbox is large.
- Capture important sender names and relationships (save to memory for future reference).

### Composing Email
1. Ask "What should I say?" to understand the message intent.
2. Draft the email in household voice (warm, helpful, family-focused).
3. Show the draft and get confirmation before sending.
4. Never send without explicit permission.

### Household Email Conventions
- Household communication style: warm, helpful, family-focused
- Save recipient relationships to memory (e.g., "Jake's teacher is Mrs. Chen")
- Respect household email privacy conventions
